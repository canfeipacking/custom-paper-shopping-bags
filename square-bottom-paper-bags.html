Square & Flat-Bottom Paper Bags

Square-bottom paper bags stand flat and self-support on the counter, distributing heavy or awkward loads across a wide base — built for bakery, grocery, retail and bulk goods.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

What a square bottom buys you

  • Self-standing base — the bag stays upright on the counter without you holding it open.
  • Wide, flat footprint that spreads weight and stops bottles or loaves tipping on one edge.
  • Higher load capacity than a gusseted carrier of the same paper weight.
  • Full base printing for a surprise logo reveal when the bag is set down.
  • Scales up from a 500 g bakery bag to a 25 kg industrial flat-bottom sack.

A base that does real structural work

Most paper bags lean on a folded gusset and a pair of handles. A square or flat-bottom paper bag replaces that with a rigid, engineered base, so the bag carries its load through the bottom rather than the seams. That matters for dense or awkward products — bread, jars, canned goods, bottled drinks, root vegetables and bulk dry goods — that sag or tip in a standard carrier. Compare constructions in our types of paper bags guide, or spec your format in the square-bottom catalogue at canfeicn.com. For heavier-duty shopping formats see the flat-handle range.

Because the base is pre-formed, packing is faster at the counter and the bag presents cleanly on a shelf or in a delivery crate. We build retail versions in printed kraft and white board, and industrial flat-bottom sacks for flour, grain and pet food up to 25 kg. For a genuinely bulk, multi-ply heavy sack, our dedicated multiwall kraft sacks go further. Current wholesale listings are on our Alibaba flat-bottom store, and matched stand-up boxes are at paperboxsupply.com bakery boxes.

Formats that stand up under real weight

Bakeries use them to carry loaves and boxed cakes flat, grocers to load jars and cans, bottle shops and markets to move dense goods without seam failure, and industrial packers for flour, grain, sugar and pet food. If the product needs to be set down and stay put — at the counter, in a delivery, or on a shelf — a square bottom is the right construction.
