Choose by load, look and budget

  • Cotton rope — comfiest, premium, best for heavy boutique loads.
  • Ribbon / grosgrain — elegant, ideal for gifts and jewellery.
  • Twisted paper cord — strong, recyclable, great value.
  • Flat glued paper — cheapest, fast to open, slim stack.
  • Die-cut / patch — minimalist, no protruding handle.

The handle is a comfort and cost decision

Handles are where premium feel and practical strength meet. A rope-handle shopping bag carries weight comfortably and reads as boutique; a twisted-handle carry bag is strong and eco; a flat or die-cut handle keeps a slim, modern profile. Attachment & reinforcement

How a handle is fixed matters as much as what it's made of. Rope and ribbon pass through a hole and are anchored by an inner patch or knot; twisted and flat paper handles are glued to the inside. For heavy loads we add a reinforced inner patch so the handle can't tear out — essential for wine bags and large square-bottom bags. Material choice interacts with handle strength — see paper weights.
