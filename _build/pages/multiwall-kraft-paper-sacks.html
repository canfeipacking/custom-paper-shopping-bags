Where multiwall earns its keep

  • 2–4 kraft plies laminated for puncture and burst strength that single-wall cannot match.
  • Moisture & grease liners — PE, kraft-laminate or barrier films for powders and hygroscopic goods.
  • Valve, open-mouth or sewn closures to run at high speed on your filling line.
  • Reliable palletising — square-block sacks that stack tight and shrink-wrap without slipping.
  • Print on the outer ply in up to 3 colours with batch, barcode and handling marks.

Bulk packaging built for the fill line and the warehouse

A multiwall sack is engineered like a shipping container, not a shopping bag. Ours laminate two to four plies of kraft — sometimes with a barrier or PE liner — so they survive filling, pneumatic conveying, palletising and weeks in a humid warehouse without burst or sifting. They are the default for flour and mixes, roasted and green coffee, animal feed, spices, chemicals, cement and minerals. Where the load is heavy but still hand-carried at retail, our flat-bottom bags fill the gap; for a lighter food-grade carrier see kraft pouch bags. The workhorse for powders and granules

Mills and food processors pack flour, sugar, cocoa and milk powder; roasters ship green and roasted coffee; agriculture uses them for feed, seed and fertiliser; and industrial suppliers bag chemicals, minerals, cement and construction mixes. Anywhere a heavy, free-flowing product needs strength, moisture protection and fast automated filling, a multiwall sack is the standard answer.
