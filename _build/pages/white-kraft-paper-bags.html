The case for white kraft

  • Bright, even surface that prints logos and small type far sharper than brown kraft.
  • Kraft-level strength — long-fibre bleached kraft resists tearing under real retail weight.
  • Recyclable and low-plastic — a cleaner eco story than glossy coated board bags.
  • Neutral canvas that suits minimalist branding, spot colour, blind deboss and one-hit foil.
  • Food-safe options with an uncoated or grease-resistant inner for bakery and take-away.

Where eco credentials meet print quality

Brands often face a trade: recyclable kraft that looks rustic, or a crisp coated board that prints beautifully but carries a heavier environmental footprint. White kraft paper bags close that gap. The bleached long-fibre surface takes fine type, spot colour and light foil cleanly, while the whole bag stays a single recyclable fibre. Pair a flat paper handle for a slim premium feel or a twisted cord for a friendly retail carry, and browse the printed formats in the white kraft catalogue at canfeicn.com. Full-colour artwork prints true with minimal ink coverage, which keeps per-unit cost down at volume. Bright, brandable bags for clean-label retail

White kraft is the go-to for brands with a minimalist identity — skincare, pharmacy, boutique food, café and bakery chains, and mid-market fashion that wants an eco message without a rustic look. The pale surface lets a small logo carry the whole design, and a single spot colour or blind-debossed wordmark reads as considered rather than cheap.
