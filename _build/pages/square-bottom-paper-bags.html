What a square bottom buys you

  • Self-standing base — the bag stays upright on the counter without you holding it open.
  • Wide, flat footprint that spreads weight and stops bottles or loaves tipping on one edge.
  • Higher load capacity than a gusseted carrier of the same paper weight.
  • Full base printing for a surprise logo reveal when the bag is set down.
  • Scales up from a 500 g bakery bag to a 25 kg industrial flat-bottom sack.

A base that does real structural work

Most paper bags lean on a folded gusset and a pair of handles. A square or flat-bottom paper bag replaces that with a rigid, engineered base, so the bag carries its load through the bottom rather than the seams. That matters for dense or awkward products — bread, jars, canned goods, bottled drinks, root vegetables and bulk dry goods — that sag or tip in a standard carrier.
