The pillars of a credible eco bag

  • FSC®-certified fibre — traceable, responsibly managed forests.
  • Recycled content — post-consumer kraft reduces virgin fibre.
  • Water-based inks — cleaner than solvent-based printing.
  • Paper handles — keep the whole bag fibre-based and recyclable.
  • Design for end-of-life — avoid laminates that hinder recycling.

Sustainability you can substantiate

Green claims invite scrutiny, so buyers need paper bags they can actually back up. The strongest signals are FSC® certification (chain-of-custody from forest to bag), recycled fibre content, water-based inks and mono-material construction. Kraft is naturally the eco favourite — see kraft characteristics and the wider case in paper vs plastic.

How to spec a genuinely green bag

Start with FSC or recycled kraft, keep it uncoated, print one or two colours with water-based inks, and fit a twisted paper handle from handle styles. That combination is strong, cheap and fully recyclable. If you need a food barrier, add a compostable liner via food-grade bags. Material trade-offs are in the materials guide.

Don't greenwash: only claim FSC if the order runs on FSC chain-of-custody stock — we'll confirm the correct certification level for your quantity.
