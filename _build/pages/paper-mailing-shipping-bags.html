Why shippers switch to paper mailers

  • Plastic-free & kerbside recyclable — a paper mailer your customer can bin with the cardboard.
  • Tear-resistant walls reinforced to survive sorting belts, drops and long-haul transit.
  • Tamper-evident adhesive strip with a re-seal option for returns without a second mailer.
  • Full brand surface — print inside and out so unboxing sells you before the product is seen.
  • Lightweight, so it adds almost nothing to dimensional-weight postage versus a rigid box.

The mailer that survives the network and the unboxing

E-commerce packaging has to do two jobs: survive a courier network that will never treat it gently, and land looking like a brand made it. Our paper mailing and shipping bags are built from reinforced kraft board that resists tears and punctures through sorting and transit, then printed edge-to-edge for a strong unboxing. They suit apparel, soft goods, books, cosmetics and subscription refills, and they close the loop on plastic poly-mailers. See the size and GSM matrix in the paper mailing bags catalogue at canfeicn.com, or scale a heavier product up to a rigid mailer from our luxury gift boxes.

The adhesive system is a real engineering choice: permanent tamper-evident strips for one-way fulfilment, or a peel-and-reseal double strip so a customer can return in the same mailer. We stock both, print custom security text on the strip, and support FSC-certified board for brands reporting on packaging footprint. Compare your options in the materials guide, and check current stock and bulk terms on our Alibaba mailing-bag store.
