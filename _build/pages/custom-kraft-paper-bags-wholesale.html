Why kraft is the default shopping bag

Kraft paper bags strike the best balance of strength, sustainability and print value, which is why cafés, boutiques, grocery chains and fashion retailers reach for them first. The natural brown finish already signals "eco", and a crisp one-colour logo is eye-catching and inexpensive.
