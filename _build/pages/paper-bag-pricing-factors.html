The four levers of price

  • Size — more paper and larger tooling = higher unit cost.
  • Material & gsm — heavier board and speciality papers cost more.
  • Quantity — bigger runs drop price per bag sharply.
  • Print & finish — colour count, foil, emboss and lamination add cost.
  • Handle — rope and ribbon cost more than flat or twisted paper.

Why two "same" bags can cost very differently

Buyers are often surprised that two similar-looking bags have different quotes. Almost always it's one of four levers: size, paper weight (gsm), quantity, or decoration. A 120 gsm kraft bag with one colour and a flat handle is the cheapest; a 300 gsm coated board bag with CMYK print, soft-touch lamination and a cotton rope is the priciest. MOQ and how to lower your cost

MOQ (minimum order quantity) is typically 500–1,000 printed bags; plain kraft can go lower. Because setup is fixed, spreading it over more units is the single biggest saving — a 5,000-run is far cheaper per bag than a 1,000-run. Other wins: use standard sizes, limit colours, drop lamination where not needed, and order a year's supply of a hero SKU.
