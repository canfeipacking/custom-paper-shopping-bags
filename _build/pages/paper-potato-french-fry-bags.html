What a great fry bag has to do

  • Steam-wise design that keeps fries and chips crisp instead of going soggy in the bag.
  • Fast scoop-and-drop open-top formats that keep a busy counter moving.
  • Bold flexo printing that carries your brand at the fairground, kiosk and takeaway window.
