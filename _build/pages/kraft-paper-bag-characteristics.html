What kraft brings to the bag

  • High tear & tensile strength — strong even at low gsm.
  • Renewable fibre — from softwood pulp, FSC®-certifiable.
  • Recyclable & biodegradable — no lamination needed.
  • Prints 1–2 colours beautifully; white kraft takes CMYK too.
  • Naturally premium eco look shoppers already trust.

Why "kraft" means strong

Kraft gets its name from the German word for strength — the sulphate pulping process leaves long, tough fibres that resist tearing and bursting far better than ordinary paper at the same weight. That's why a thin kraft bag can carry a heavy grocery load. The same fibre makes it ideal for grocery, food and multiwall sacks. Compare it to coated board in the materials guide.

Both natural brown and white kraft are available, in virgin and recycled grades. See the range on our kraft characteristics guide at canfeicn.com and kraft bag catalogue.

Properties at a glance

PropertyKraft paper
Tensile / tear strengthVery high for its weight — the key advantage.
Common gsm120–200 gsm for bags; 110–170 for lightweight carriers.
ColourNatural brown or bleached white; recycled grey-brown.
Print behaviourBest for 1–2 spot colours; white kraft suits CMYK.
RigidityLower than coated board — softer, flexible hand feel.
SustainabilityRenewable, recyclable, biodegradable, FSC®-certifiable.
CostAmong the lowest per bag for its strength.

When to choose kraft (and when not)

Pick kraft when you want strength, sustainability and value — retail, grocery, takeaway and eco-led brands. Choose coated board instead when you need a rigid structure or full-photographic print, or black board for foil drama. To weigh this against your budget, see pricing factors; for the eco case, paper vs plastic.

Colour note: natural kraft has a warm tone that shifts how inks look — proof on the real paper, not white, for accurate brand colour.
