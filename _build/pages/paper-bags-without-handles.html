Why drop the handle

  • Clean flat profile that slides into drawers, boxes, envelopes and packing lines without bulk.
  • Lower unit cost — no handle stock, no hand-tying, faster machine assembly.
  • Full 360-degree print with no handle holes or patches interrupting the artwork.
  • Perfect for wrapping — a product slip-bag, banding sleeve or printed insert carrier.
  • Stacks and ships flatter, cutting your freight and warehousing cost per thousand.

Less hardware, more design surface

A handle is useful for carrying, but sometimes it gets in the way. Our paper bags without handles are built for products that are wrapped, inserted, folded or machine-packed — a kraft slip-bag for a folded garment, a printed sleeve for an art print or menu, a flat gift carrier that a shop finishes with its own ribbon. Removing the handle lowers cost per unit, lets the artwork run uninterrupted edge to edge, and lets the bag pack flat. Explore the printed wrap formats in our custom printed bags series, and see the handle-free range in the canfeicn.com no-handle catalogue.

Because there is no handle patch, we design these formats around fold, glue and score lines instead — so the choice of paper and grammage matters more. A lighter 90–120 gsm kraft suits single-use wrapping; a 200 gsm white board suits a reusable flat gift bag. Where a flat bag beats a handled one

These formats earn their place in apparel and textile wrapping, printed inserts and lookbooks, stationery and art prints, bakery and deli flat packs, and any automated packing line where a handle would jam the machine. They are also a favourite for shops that prefer to add their own seasonal ribbon rather than a fixed handle.
