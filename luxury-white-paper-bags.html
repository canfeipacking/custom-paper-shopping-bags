Luxury White Paper Bags

Crisp, high-end white coated-board bags made for boutiques, jewellery, cosmetics and hotels — finished with foil, deboss or soft-touch and your choice of ribbon or rope handle.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Built for a premium counter experience

  • 250–300 gsm coated art board for a rigid, high-end hand feel.
  • Foil & deboss ready — gold, silver, rose-gold or blind-embossed logo.
  • Soft-touch, matte or gloss lamination with reinforced base.
  • Ribbon, cotton-rope or grosgrain handles hand-tied for boutiques.
  • Spot UV & anti-scratch film options for a truly luxury finish.

White board bags that elevate your brand

When the unboxing is part of the product, a luxury white paper bag does the heavy lifting. Our coated art-board bags hold their shape, print photographic detail beautifully, and take foil and debossing like a flagship boutique expects. Pair them with a satin ribbon for jewellery or a cotton rope for fashion — and see the same luxury formats in our white paper bag catalogue.

Because we laminate, foil and finish in-house, we control colour accuracy and edge quality that trading companies can't. Explore matched gift paper bags and rigid gift boxes to build a complete premium set, or view live wholesale listings on our Alibaba store and paperboxsupply.com.

Best suited to

These bags shine where presentation sells: jewellery and watch counters, cosmetics and fragrance boutiques, wedding and gift retail, hotels and corporate gifting. Choose a matte black alternative for a darker luxury mood, or keep the full range consistent with our printed paper bags.
