How to Customize Paper Shopping Bags

Everything a buyer needs to spec a custom paper shopping bag the first time right — size, material, handle, print, finish, MOQ and sampling — in one clear walkthrough.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

The six decisions that define your bag

  • Size & capacity — width × height × gusset, matched to what it carries.
  • Material — kraft, white kraft or coated board by strength and budget.
  • Handle — rope, ribbon, twisted, flat or die-cut for the right feel.
  • Print & finish — CMYK/PMS, then lamination, foil or emboss.
  • MOQ & sampling — set quantity, then approve a sample before bulk.

Customizing is simple once you know the levers

A custom paper shopping bag is defined by a handful of choices, and getting them right the first time saves weeks and money. Start from what the bag must carry, work through material and handle, then dial in printing and finishing. This guide walks the exact process we use with retail and gift brands every day.

When you're ready, send your brief for a free mock-up and firm price — or compare live options on our how-to-custom page at canfeicn.com and our verified Alibaba store. For matched boxes, see paperboxsupply.com.

Step by step

1. Define size & capacity

Measure the tallest, widest and deepest item, add room, then set width × height × gusset. Use our size chart for common retail formats.

2. Choose the material

Natural or white kraft for eco value; 250–300 gsm coated board for luxury. See choosing bag material.

3. Select the handle

Cotton rope or ribbon for boutiques, twisted paper for everyday, flat or die-cut for a slim profile. Compare in handle styles.

4. Decide print & finish

CMYK for photos, PMS spot colour for exact brand match, then matte/gloss lamination, foil or emboss. Full detail in printing & finishes.

5. Confirm MOQ & quote

Typically 500–1,000 pcs. Price scales with size, gsm, colour count and quantity — see pricing factors.

6. Approve a sample

Check colour, structure and handle on a physical or digital sample before we run bulk.

Five mistakes that cost buyers money

The most common issues are under-sizing the bag for its contents, choosing thin board for a heavy product, sending low-resolution artwork, ignoring gsm when comparing prices, and skipping the physical sample for a colour-critical brand. Each is avoidable: spec from the product, match material to load, send vectors, compare like-for-like gsm, and always approve a sample. If you're unsure, our team will advise on the best material and weight for your use case at no cost.
