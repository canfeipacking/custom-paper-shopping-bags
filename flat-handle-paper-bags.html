Flat-Handle Paper Bags

A clean, low-profile carrier with flat or die-cut patch handles — minimalist branding, comfortable grip and a tidy stack, printed on kraft or coated board.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Minimal look, practical carry

  • Flat or die-cut patch handles for a clean silhouette.
  • Lies flat in storage and ships efficiently.
  • Reinforced handle patches for dependable grip.
  • Kraft or coated board to match your brand.
  • Full-colour or one-colour custom printing.

Understated and hard-working

Flat-handle paper bags suit brands that want a tidy, modern look without a protruding rope. Die-cut patch handles keep the profile slim and the stack compact — ideal for fashion, cosmetics and takeaway. Explore handle options in our flat paper bag catalogue, and see handle & rope styles to compare grips.

For a premium boutique carry step to rope shopping bags; for volume retail see carry bags. Live wholesale terms on our Alibaba store.
