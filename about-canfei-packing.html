Who we are

A paper bag factory you can build on

Canfei Packing (Shenzhen Canfei Packing Co., Ltd.) has spent more than 15 years making custom paper shopping bags, gift bags and packaging for brands worldwide. We own our presses, laminators and finishing lines, so quality, colour and price stay under one roof — no trading-company middleman.

We specialise in custom paper shopping bags and the full family of paper packaging — kraft, luxury white, black, food-grade and gift bags — plus matched rigid gift boxes. Our export team handles artwork, sampling, production and shipping so you can reorder with confidence.

Why it matters

What owning the factory gets you

Because we control every step, buyers get tighter colour accuracy, dependable handle placement, honest gsm and faster turnarounds — and the pricing reflects a real factory, not a reseller margin. From the first customization brief to the delivered pallet, one team owns your order.

  • In-house offset printing, foil, emboss & lamination
  • Quality control on raw material, print, glue and handle pull
  • Low MOQ and free design for launches and test runs
  • FOB / CIF / DDP shipping with export documents handled
