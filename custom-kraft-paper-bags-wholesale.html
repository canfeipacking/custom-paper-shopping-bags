Custom Kraft Paper Bags

The world's favourite eco carrier bag — strong, recyclable and instantly brandable. Natural brown or clean white kraft, printed with your logo and finished with paper or cotton handles.

The eco workhorse — key strengths

  • 100% recyclable & biodegradable natural or white kraft, water-based inks.
  • FSC®-certifiable fibre for credible sustainability claims.
  • Strong & cheap — high tear resistance at the lowest cost per bag.
  • Handles your way — twisted paper cord, cotton rope or flat cut-out.
  • 1–4 colour or full CMYK print, from a single logo to edge-to-edge artwork.

Why kraft is the default shopping bag

Kraft paper bags strike the best balance of strength, sustainability and print value, which is why cafés, boutiques, grocery chains and fashion retailers reach for them first. The natural brown finish already signals "eco", and a crisp one-colour logo is eye-catching and inexpensive. For a cleaner premium look, white kraft takes full-colour print beautifully without a heavy coated-board cost. Browse the same constructions across our kraft paper bag catalogue.

Order natural tones from our natural brown kraft range, or go bright-white with our white kraft paper bags. Need the same bag for food? Our food-grade kraft bags add a grease-resistant option. Common uses

Kraft shopping bags are the do-everything carrier: retail and boutique purchases, grocery and takeaway, event giveaways and sustainable brand launches. Because they print well in one or two colours, they are the most cost-effective way to put your logo on thousands of bags. For heavier or luxury needs, step across to our rope-handle shopping bags or SOS bags.
