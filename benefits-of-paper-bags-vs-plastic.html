Paper Bags vs Plastic

Plastic-bag bans and eco-conscious shoppers have made paper the default. Here's the honest case for paper bags — benefits, trade-offs and how to spec a truly sustainable bag.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Why brands choose paper

  • Recyclable & biodegradable — fibre-based, widely recovered.
  • Renewable — made from managed forest pulp, FSC®-certifiable.
  • Premium brand canvas — prints beautifully, feels considered.
  • Regulation-proof — ahead of single-use plastic bans worldwide.
  • Shopper trust — paper signals sustainability at the counter.

The switch is already happening

From the EU to US states and Asian cities, single-use plastic is being restricted, and shoppers increasingly judge brands by packaging. Paper bags answer both pressures: they're recyclable, renewable and — unlike thin plastic — they carry your brand well. The eco credentials of the material itself are covered in FSC & eco certifications, and the strengths of the most common paper in kraft bag characteristics.

Choose the right format and the eco story holds: kraft for value, food-grade for counters, coated shopping bags for premium. See the range on our advantages-of-paper-bags guide at canfeicn.com and Alibaba store.

Paper vs plastic, honestly

FactorPaper vs plastic
RecyclabilityPaper is widely recycled; many plastics aren't collected.
BiodegradingPaper breaks down far faster than plastic in the environment.
SourcePaper is renewable fibre; plastic is fossil-based.
BrandingPaper prints premium & tactile; plastic looks commodity.
Water resistancePlastic wins when wet — paper needs a liner or grease/barrier.
Strength/weightGood kraft is strong for its weight; heavy loads need reinforced bases.
CostThin plastic is cheapest; kraft is close and often better value per brand impression.

The trade-offs to get right

Paper isn't perfect: it loses strength when wet and its footprint depends on sourcing and weight. The fix is smart spec — FSC-certified or recycled fibre, the lowest gsm that safely carries the load, water-based inks and paper handles for a fully recyclable bag. For wet or oily use, add a barrier via grease-proof bags. Read how to choose material to balance strength and sustainability.

Biggest win: a right-sized kraft bag with paper handles and one-colour print is recyclable, cheap and on-brand — the sweet spot for most retailers replacing plastic.
