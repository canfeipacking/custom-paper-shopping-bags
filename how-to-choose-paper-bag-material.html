How to Choose Paper Bag Material

Choose the right paper bag material by working through four questions — load, look, budget and eco goal — then match kraft, white kraft, coated or black board to your use.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Answer four questions, pick the paper

  • How heavy? → sets gsm and whether you need board rigidity.
  • What look? → natural kraft, clean white or premium coated.
  • What budget? → kraft is cheapest; coated & speciality cost more.
  • What eco goal? → recycled, FSC® or uncoated fully recyclable.
  • Then confirm print — some papers suit CMYK, others only 1–2 colours.

Material is a balance, not a single "best"

There's no universally best paper — only the best fit for your load, look, budget and eco goal. Natural kraft wins on strength-per-dollar and sustainability; coated board wins on rigidity and photographic print; black board wins on foil drama. The full property comparison is in our materials guide, and kraft's specifics in kraft characteristics.

We'll send swatches and advise. Compare materials on our choose-material guide at canfeicn.com, and read about coated paper for premium print.

The decision framework

1. Load first

Light retail → 120–140 gsm kraft; heavy groceries → 170 gsm kraft; rigid premium → 250–300 gsm board.

2. Look & print

Warm eco → natural kraft; clean brand → white kraft; vivid photo/foil → coated or black board.

3. Budget

Kraft + 1–2 colours is the value zone; coated + lamination + foil is the premium zone. See pricing.

4. Eco goal

Recycled or FSC® kraft with paper handles = fully recyclable. Lamination adds feel but can reduce recyclability.
