Pricing & MOQ Factors

Custom paper bag price comes down to four levers — size, material weight, quantity and decoration. Here's how each moves cost, what MOQ means, and how to get the best price.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

The four levers of price

  • Size — more paper and larger tooling = higher unit cost.
  • Material & gsm — heavier board and speciality papers cost more.
  • Quantity — bigger runs drop price per bag sharply.
  • Print & finish — colour count, foil, emboss and lamination add cost.
  • Handle — rope and ribbon cost more than flat or twisted paper.

Why two "same" bags can cost very differently

Buyers are often surprised that two similar-looking bags have different quotes. Almost always it's one of four levers: size, paper weight (gsm), quantity, or decoration. A 120 gsm kraft bag with one colour and a flat handle is the cheapest; a 300 gsm coated board bag with CMYK print, soft-touch lamination and a cotton rope is the priciest. Understand material first via the gsm guide, then decoration via printing options.

How each factor moves cost

FactorEffect on price · how to optimise
SizeBigger = more paper. Use the smallest format that fits the product.
Material / gsmHeavier & speciality cost more. Match gsm to load, don't over-spec.
QuantityUnit cost falls with volume. Consolidate SKUs to reach a price break.
Colour countEach extra PMS colour adds setup. Prefer 1–2 colours or CMYK.
FinishesFoil, emboss, spot UV, lamination each add a step. Pick 1–2 key effects.
HandleRope/ribbon > twisted > flat. Choose by need, not habit.
ToolingStandard sizes share tooling; bespoke adds a one-off cost.

MOQ and how to lower your cost

MOQ (minimum order quantity) is typically 500–1,000 printed bags; plain kraft can go lower. Because setup is fixed, spreading it over more units is the single biggest saving — a 5,000-run is far cheaper per bag than a 1,000-run. Other wins: use standard sizes, limit colours, drop lamination where not needed, and order a year's supply of a hero SKU. The trade-offs are set against size and material in the size chart and materials guide.

Get the best price: send size, quantity, material and artwork and we'll return a tiered quote (e.g. 1k / 5k / 10k) so you can see exactly where the savings are.
