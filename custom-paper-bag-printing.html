Printing & Finishing Options

The print and finish decide how premium your bag looks. Compare CMYK and PMS offset, silk-screen, hot foil, deboss, spot UV and lamination — and pick the right combo for your brand.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Decoration, ranked by impact

  • CMYK offset — full-colour photos & gradients on any board.
  • PMS spot colour — exact, repeatable brand colours.
  • Hot foil — gold, silver, rose or holographic metallic logo.
  • Deboss / emboss — tactile raised or pressed logo.
  • Lamination & spot UV — matte, gloss, soft-touch and gloss-on-matte.

Print method vs finish — two separate choices

Colour and finish are different decisions that combine. First choose how the logo is printed (CMYK for imagery, PMS for exact brand colour, silk-screen for bold spot coverage), then how the surface is finished (lamination, foil, emboss, spot UV). A white board bag with PMS logo plus soft-touch lamination reads premium; a black board bag with gold foil is the classic luxury move. See the range printed on our custom logo & printing page at canfeicn.com.

For matched box printing, compare effects on paperboxsupply.com special effects, and browse live printed bags on our Alibaba store.

LaminationMatte, gloss or soft-touch film · protection & feel
Spot UVGloss detail on matte surface · subtle highlights

Winning combinations

The most requested premium recipes are: matte lamination + blind deboss (understated luxury), soft-touch + foil logo (jewellery/cosmetics), and gloss + spot UV (vibrant retail). For eco brands, one-colour PMS on natural kraft with no lamination keeps the bag fully recyclable — see FSC & eco options. Colour-count is also a key cost lever, explained in pricing factors.

Artwork tip: supply vector files and a separate layer for foil/emboss so we can register the effects precisely to your logo.
