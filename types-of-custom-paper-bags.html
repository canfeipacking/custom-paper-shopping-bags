Types of Custom Paper Bags

Not sure which paper bag you actually need? This guide maps every custom paper bag type — by handle, structure and use — so you can shortlist fast and order right.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Three ways to classify a paper bag

  • By handle — rope, ribbon, twisted, flat, die-cut or none.
  • By structure — shopping, SOS, square-bottom, pouch, sack or mailing.
  • By material — kraft, white kraft, coated board or food-grade.
  • By use — retail, grocery, gift, food, wine or shipping.
  • Most bags combine all three — e.g. a rope-handle coated-board shopping bag.

Find your bag type in 60 seconds

"Paper bag" covers a huge family of products, and the right type depends on what you carry, how premium it must feel and your budget. A boutique needs a rope-handle shopping bag; a deli needs food-grade bags; a webshop needs mailing bags. Use the map below to jump to the exact type, then spec it with our customization guide.

How to shortlist

Ask three questions: what goes in it (weight & shape sets structure and gsm), who holds it (a gift or boutique wants rope/ribbon; a checkout wants flat/SOS), and what must it say (eco → kraft; premium → coated board + foil). For the material decision see choosing bag material; for the sustainability angle see paper vs plastic.
