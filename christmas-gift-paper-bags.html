Christmas Gift Paper Bags

Festive red and green paper gift bags with foil detailing, ribbon handles and seasonal print — engineered for boutique hampers, wine sets and holiday retail drops.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

What makes these holiday bags sell

  • Seasonal palettes — deep pine, berry red, kraft natural and ivory, all built for metallic foil.
  • Foil and emboss detail on snowflakes, reindeer, trees, holly, monograms and greeting typography.
  • Ribbon or twisted-cord handles in burgundy, bottle-green, or red-and-white bakers twine.
  • Built for hampers and sets — sizes tested against common wine, candle and gift-box footprints.
  • Q4-secure production so foil and hand-tying slots close early but reliably land before December.

A festive bag that finishes the gift before it is wrapped

Christmas retail packaging lives or dies on the unboxing moment. Our custom Christmas gift paper bags combine a printed red, green or kraft body with foil-stamped motifs, matching rope handles and a stiffened base sized for wine, candles, cosmetics and apparel gift sets. Match the bag colour and print system to the ribbon on your hamper, or coordinate the deckle edge with our rigid gift boxes to sell a full holiday set. The complete festive range is live at canfeicn.com Christmas paper bag catalogue.

Order timing matters more than unit price in Q4. Foil tooling, ribbon sourcing and hand-tying capacity all close out by mid-year, so we book Christmas production from August and support late-window reorders with air freight. Current stock lots and terms sit on our Alibaba Christmas listings. For a lighter eco-festive brief, the natural kraft series accepts the same one-colour red or green foil at a lower unit cost, and holiday gift boxes at paperboxsupply.com round out the same print system.

Where these Christmas bags perform best

Boutique retail, hotel gifting programmes, corporate hampers and D2C brands running a November–December drop all use these bags as the outermost brand touchpoint — they need to sit cleanly on a checkout counter, look right next to a wrapped gift, and survive a full day of shopping. Because the base is stiffened, the same bag doubles as a wine, bottle or candle carrier without an extra insert.
