Multiwall Kraft Paper Sacks

Multi-ply kraft paper sacks engineered for bulk powders and granules — flour, coffee, feed, chemicals and building products — from 5 to 25 kg with liners, valves and high-strength stitching.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Where multiwall earns its keep

  • 2–4 kraft plies laminated for puncture and burst strength that single-wall cannot match.
  • Moisture & grease liners — PE, kraft-laminate or barrier films for powders and hygroscopic goods.
  • Valve, open-mouth or sewn closures to run at high speed on your filling line.
  • Reliable palletising — square-block sacks that stack tight and shrink-wrap without slipping.
  • Print on the outer ply in up to 3 colours with batch, barcode and handling marks.

Bulk packaging built for the fill line and the warehouse

A multiwall sack is engineered like a shipping container, not a shopping bag. Ours laminate two to four plies of kraft — sometimes with a barrier or PE liner — so they survive filling, pneumatic conveying, palletising and weeks in a humid warehouse without burst or sifting. They are the default for flour and mixes, roasted and green coffee, animal feed, spices, chemicals, cement and minerals. Where the load is heavy but still hand-carried at retail, our flat-bottom bags fill the gap; for a lighter food-grade carrier see kraft pouch bags. Full ply and liner options are in the multiwall sack catalogue at canfeicn.com.

Because these run at volume, we focus on consistency: same burst factor, same liner weight, same fill-mouth tolerances order after order, so your line does not jam or reject. We supply sift-test and stacking-strength data, and can match a valve to your wicket or sewing head. Check the structural difference from a single-wall bag in our types-of-bags guide, and request mill-bulk terms on our Alibaba industrial store.

The workhorse for powders and granules

Mills and food processors pack flour, sugar, cocoa and milk powder; roasters ship green and roasted coffee; agriculture uses them for feed, seed and fertiliser; and industrial suppliers bag chemicals, minerals, cement and construction mixes. Anywhere a heavy, free-flowing product needs strength, moisture protection and fast automated filling, a multiwall sack is the standard answer.
