Kraft Paper Grocery Bags

High-volume grocery and market carriers — reinforced kraft bags that hold weight, stack flat and print your brand affordably for supermarkets, delis and farmers' markets.

Built for weight and volume

  • Reinforced base carries heavy groceries and bottles safely.
  • Twisted or flat handles for fast, comfortable carrying.
  • High tensile kraft resists tears and dynamic loads.
  • Bulk pricing for supermarkets, chains and markets.
  • 100% recyclable — the plastic-bag replacement shoppers expect.

The grocery bag, done right

Grocery and market bags live or die on base strength and price per unit. Our kraft grocery bags use high-tensile paper and a glued reinforced base to carry heavy, awkward loads, and they ship flat to keep freight low. Match them with our food bags for deli counters, or see the range in our grocery bag catalogue.

For a lighter everyday carrier see paper carry bags; for a premium market-stall look, add print via our printed bags. Live bulk terms are on our Alibaba store and wholesale shopping bag guide.
