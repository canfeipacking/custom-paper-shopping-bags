Safe for food, kind to the planet

  • Food-grade kraft suitable for direct food contact.

Packaging that keeps food fresh and brands visible

From bakery counters to street food, food-safe kraft bags protect product while advertising your brand. We supply grease-resistant and plain food-grade kraft in flat, gusseted and window formats, printed with food-safe inks.
