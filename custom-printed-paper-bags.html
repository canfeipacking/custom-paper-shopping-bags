Print that makes the bag the marketing

A custom printed paper bag is a moving billboard. Our offset presses reproduce logos, patterns and photography with crisp registration across the whole surface, so a simple brand mark or a busy all-over print both come out sharp. Compare the effect on warm kraft versus a clean white board, and see live examples in our printed paper bag catalogue.

From single-colour logos to full-wrap artwork, we proof free and print in-house for consistent colour on repeat orders. Explore current printed designs on our Alibaba store, or pair printed bags with printed boxes at paperboxsupply.com.

Popular applications
