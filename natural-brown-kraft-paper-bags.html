Natural Brown Kraft Paper Bags

Plain and printed natural brown kraft bags built for grocery, coffee, bakery, take-away and everyday retail — the highest recycled-content carrier in our range.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Why brands standardise on natural kraft

  • True unbleached fibre — the warm brown tone is the paper itself, so there is no dyelot shift between orders.
  • SOS twisted-cord handles machine-inserted at speed, giving a strong carry with no glue line.
  • Whole-bag recyclability — paper body, paper handle, water-based ink, no plastic barrier to separate.
  • 90–180 gsm range from light sandwich bags to heavy 20 kg-capable retail carriers.
  • Lowest cost per bag in our custom range once you move to single-colour flexo printing.

The honest carrier for brands that mean it about plastic

Natural brown kraft has become the visual shorthand for sustainable retail, and our unbleached kraft paper bags lean into that with a fibre-forward finish that needs no lamination to look intentional. Choose an SOS string-handle format for high-throughput grocery and café counters, or step up to a reinforced grocery carrier when basket weights climb. Every size, gsm and print option is listed in the natural kraft catalogue at canfeicn.com, and the environmental case for switching away from single-use plastic is set out in our paper versus plastic guide.

Because the base paper is one of the few substrates we keep in continuous roll stock, repeat orders stay cheap and fast. One-colour or two-colour flexo prints run at volume pricing, and a bare-brown bag with a single PMS logo is the most cost-effective way to carry a brand across hundreds of stores. Live wholesale listings are on our Alibaba kraft bag store, and matching recycled boxes are at paperboxsupply.com kraft boxes. Not sure which weight fits your product? Built for high-volume, eco-led counters

Natural kraft is the default where bags are grabbed by the hundred: supermarket delis, coffee and bakery chains, quick-service take-away, zero-refill shops and market stalls. The plain-brown surface photographs well, hides fingerprints and grease edges, and reads as sustainable before a single word of branding is printed, so a minimal one-colour logo is often all a store needs.
