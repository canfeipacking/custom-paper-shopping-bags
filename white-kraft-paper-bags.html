White Kraft Paper Bags

White kraft bags give you the recyclable strength of kraft with a bright, clean surface that makes logos and colour pop — ideal for retail, bakery and premium take-away.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

The case for white kraft

  • Bright, even surface that prints logos and small type far sharper than brown kraft.
  • Kraft-level strength — long-fibre bleached kraft resists tearing under real retail weight.
  • Recyclable and low-plastic — a cleaner eco story than glossy coated board bags.
  • Neutral canvas that suits minimalist branding, spot colour, blind deboss and one-hit foil.
  • Food-safe options with an uncoated or grease-resistant inner for bakery and take-away.

Where eco credentials meet print quality

Brands often face a trade: recyclable kraft that looks rustic, or a crisp coated board that prints beautifully but carries a heavier environmental footprint. White kraft paper bags close that gap. The bleached long-fibre surface takes fine type, spot colour and light foil cleanly, while the whole bag stays a single recyclable fibre. Pair a flat paper handle for a slim premium feel or a twisted cord for a friendly retail carry, and browse the printed formats in the white kraft catalogue at canfeicn.com. For a heavier coated alternative with more sheen, see our luxury white board bags.

We keep white kraft in three weights so you can match cost to product — a light 120 gsm for bakery and small retail, a 160 gsm all-rounder, and a heavy 200 gsm+ carrier for bottles and apparel. Full-colour artwork prints true with minimal ink coverage, which keeps per-unit cost down at volume. Live bulk listings are on our Alibaba white kraft store, and to understand how the paper itself behaves with your logo, read the material selection guide.

Bright, brandable bags for clean-label retail

White kraft is the go-to for brands with a minimalist identity — skincare, pharmacy, boutique food, café and bakery chains, and mid-market fashion that wants an eco message without a rustic look. The pale surface lets a small logo carry the whole design, and a single spot colour or blind-debossed wordmark reads as considered rather than cheap.
