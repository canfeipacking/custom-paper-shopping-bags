SOS String-Handle Paper Bags

SOS (Self-Open Satchel) bags open with one motion and lock flat at the base — the fast, sturdy, stackable carrier retail and takeaway crews love, printed with your brand.

One-motion open, all-day carry

  • Self-opening base pops open instantly for fast packing.
  • String or flat handles for comfortable carrying.
  • Stacks flat in storage and ships efficiently.
  • Strong kraft with a glued reinforced base.

The speed-and-strength carrier

SOS bags (Self-Open Satchel) are the checkout favourite: crews open them in one motion, the base locks flat, and they carry surprisingly well. We make them in kraft with string or flat handles, printed to spec. See our SOS bag catalogue, and compare with flat-handle and carry bags.

For food counters pair with food-grade bags; check live wholesale on our Alibaba store and paperboxsupply.com.
