Paper Bag Materials & Colors

The paper you choose sets strength, print quality, sustainability and price. This guide compares every material we use — with gsm ranges, best uses and colour options.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

Material at a glance

  • Natural kraft — strongest for weight, cheapest, eco look, 120–200 gsm.
  • White kraft — bright printable kraft, no lamination needed, 120–200 gsm.
  • Coated art board — premium rigour & photographic print, 210–300 gsm.
  • White board (grey back) — value alternative to art board, 250–350 gsm.
  • Black card & speciality paper — luxury foil/deboss canvas.

Why material drives everything else

The paper decides how much the bag carries, how your logo prints, how it feels and what it costs. For an eco-first retail bag, kraft is unbeatable on strength-per-dollar; for a boutique counter, coated board delivers rigidity and vivid print. Read the fuller decision framework in how to choose paper bag material, and see the properties of kraft in kraft bag characteristics.

We stock every material here and can send swatches. Compare live specs on our materials & colours page at canfeicn.com, or browse board options for matched boxes at paperboxsupply.com paper options.

Understanding gsm (paper weight)

GSM (grams per square metre) is the single best price/quality comparator. A 120 gsm kraft bag is light and cheap; 170 gsm handles heavy groceries; 250–300 gsm board feels rigid and premium. Never compare two quotes without matching gsm — a "cheaper" bag is often just thinner. Our pricing guide shows how weight moves cost, and the size chart pairs weight with dimensions.

Rule of thumb: light retail → 120–140 gsm kraft; everyday shopping → 170 gsm kraft; boutique luxury → 250–300 gsm coated board.
