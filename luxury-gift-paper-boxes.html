Luxury Gift Paper Boxes

Rigid and magnetic-closure luxury gift paper boxes with foil, soft-touch and bespoke inserts — engineered to match our premium shopping bags into one branded gift set.

The gift set that sells the brand twice

  • Rigid construction from 1.5–3 mm greyboard — a box that keeps its shape and reuses well.
  • Magnetic, lid-and-base or drawer closures for a weighted, premium unboxing.
  • Matched to our shopping bags — same paper, colour, foil and logo across box and bag.
  • Bespoke inserts in EVA, sponge, flocked or card to hold jewellery, watches and bottles.
  • Full decoration menu — hot foil, soft-touch, deboss, spot UV and ribbon pulls.

A box and bag that arrive as one gift

A luxury purchase is judged in the hand before the product is even seen. Our rigid luxury gift paper boxes are built to be presented, not just packed — thick greyboard wrapped in printed or soft-touch paper, a magnetic or deep lid-and-base close, and an insert cut to cradle the exact product. The strategic advantage is coordination: choose the same wrap paper and foil as your carrier, and the box and bag become a single branded set. Start with a luxury white bag or a charcoal bag, then match the box, and browse the wider case range in the gift box catalogue at canfeicn.com.

Because we print, foil and hand-wrap boxes and bags under one roof, colour and finishing stay matched across the set — the thing that most easily goes wrong when you buy them from two suppliers. For dedicated formats, our box partner lists magnetic closure luxury gift boxes at paperboxsupply.com and jewellery boxes at paperboxsupply.com. Explore gift-bag pairings in our custom gift paper bags, and read how to brief a set in the customisation guide.

Where a rigid box earns its premium

Jewellery and watch counters, fragrance and cosmetics, premium spirits and gift sets, corporate and event gifting, and D2C brands with a signature unboxing all use rigid boxes as the definitive premium container. Matched with our shopping bags, the same design system carries the customer from storefront to shelf and makes the gift photograph beautifully.
