Charcoal & Dark Grey Paper Bags

Charcoal, graphite and true-black paper bags with a soft matte hand, foil or blind-deboss logos and premium handles — the darker, quieter alternative to gloss black.

Get a Quote WhatsApp

The premium-dark proposition

  • Soft-touch matte surface that reads as expensive and resists fingerprints better than gloss.
  • Charcoal to true-black — from warm graphite grey to a deep saturated through-dyed core.
  • Foil or blind-deboss logos that lift cleanly off a dark ground without any ink haze.
  • Rope, ribbon or flat handles in black, grey, or a single metallic accent.
  • Reinforced base so a premium-looking bag is also a genuinely heavy-load carrier.

Understated dark packaging for considered brands

A charcoal or black paper bag signals premium faster than almost any other finish, and it flatters metallic decoration the way a dark frame flatters a print. Ours are made from through-dyed board in warm graphite or deep black, finished with a soft-touch matte laminate, and decorated with gold, silver or holographic foil — or, for a subtler look, a blind-debossed wordmark that is felt rather than read. Pair the bag with a matched rigid case in our luxury gift boxes, or take the brighter route with the matte black series. The full dark range is at canfeicn.com charcoal paper bags.

Dark board is unforgiving of poor registration, so we print, laminate and foil on our own lines to keep edges clean and handles symmetrical. For a fashion, tech or spirits counter where the bag is the last brand impression, that consistency is worth more than a few cents of saving. Compare the deeper blacks against a warm grey in the materials & colour guide, and see live listings on our Alibaba dark bag store.

Where dark packaging wins the sale

Fashion boutiques, watch and jewellery counters, tech and audio launches, premium spirits, barbershops and grooming brands and event gifting all rely on dark bags to set a serious, considered tone. The charcoal variants are the quiet middle ground: less severe than true black, and kinder to everyday print runs where a deep black can show scuffs.

Tip: On a dark ground, one metallic accent outperforms a busy multi-colour print. Try a single rose-gold foil logo on soft-touch charcoal, and reserve white ink for the brand name only — it keeps the bag premium and lowers your finishing cost.

FAQ

Gloss black shows everything. We recommend a soft-touch matte laminate or an anti-scratch film, both of which hide handling marks far better — ask for a matte sample before signing off.

Metallic foil, white ink, and blind deboss all read cleanly on a dark ground. Full-colour artwork needs a white underprint to stay bright; our printing guide covers the options.
