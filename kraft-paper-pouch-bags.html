Pouches that pop on the shelf

Kraft paper pouches are the go-to for snacks, coffee, tea, bakery and dry goods — a natural look that stands out and tells an eco story. We add windows, resealable zippers and barrier liners to protect freshness, printed edge to edge. See formats in our paper pouch catalogue, and pair with food bags for a full counter range.
