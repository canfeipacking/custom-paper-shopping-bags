Paper Wine Bottle Bags

Purpose-built wine carriers with reinforced bases and bottle-safe handles — the premium, giftable way to sell a bottle, from single-bottle boutique bags to 3-bottle crates.

Safe, giftable, brand-forward

  • Reinforced base supports the full weight of glass bottles.
  • 1, 2 & 3-bottle formats plus internal dividers available.
  • Rope, die-cut or partition handles sized for bottle necks.
  • Foil & full-colour print for premium gift presentation.
  • Kraft or coated board to match your brand's price point.

Make the bottle a gift before it leaves the counter

Wine buyers gift as often as they drink, and a purpose-made paper wine bag turns a single bottle into a present. Our carriers have reinforced bases, bottle-safe handles and optional dividers, printed on kraft for value or coated board for premium labels. See formats in our wine bag catalogue.
